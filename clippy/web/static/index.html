          Campaigns

          Clipping campaigns for a streamer, a podcast or a brand: the brief, the moments to clip, the clippers' posts and what each one earns.

          How it works: the client gives a brief, a rate per 1,000 views and a cap per post, plus the moments to clip. Clippy cuts every source with the brief as its picking rules, you add the clippers' post links, and Clippy reads their public views and works out the payout.

          Clip only footage you have the right to use: what a campaign owner gives you is licensed for that campaign.
